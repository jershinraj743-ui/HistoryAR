About HistoryAR

HistoryAR is an innovative Augmented Reality based educational solution designed to make history learning more engaging, visual and interactive. Students can scan textbook images using their mobile cameras and explore historical kings, monuments and objects through 3D models, animations and audio explanations.

Problem Statement

Limited Visualization

Students find it difficult to imagine ancient kings, monuments and historical events.

Traditional Learning

Textbook-based learning provides limited interactive experiences.

Lack of Interest

Text-heavy content can make history boring and less engaging.

Memory Challenges

Students may struggle to remember historical information through memorization alone.

Our Solution

HistoryAR bridges the gap between traditional education and modern technology by converting textbook images into interactive Augmented Reality experiences.

Scan Textbook

Scan historical images using a mobile camera.

AR Recognition

The application recognizes the scanned textbook images.

Explore 3D Models

View and interact with historical objects in 3D.

Audio Learning

Listen to explanations and learn historical facts easily.
